Igra Mainnet Bridge
This bridge wraps KAS from Kaspa Mainnet into iKAS on Igra Mainnet. KAS is sent to the Entry address. Minimum amount: 10 KAS. No maximum. Ensure your Kaspa wallet is set to mainnet.
